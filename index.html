The scientific story · September 2026

How far can terrestrial extremophiles take us?

ThermoBase began as a compact database. It became an evidence-linked research system for asking what environmental conditions, physiology, metabolism and evolution can tell us about life beyond Earth — and where those inferences break.

01 · Observe

Before we model anything, look at the evidence.

A literature-assembled dataset is not a census of life. Its composition, missingness and provenance determine what questions can be answered.

Who is represented?

Read this as sampling structure, not abundance in nature. Bacteria and Archaea dominate the curated evidence base.

The first question

Do recurring physiological patterns reflect environmental constraints — or the lineages that happen to occupy those environments?

02 · Physiology

A number is not automatically a fact.

Thermal envelopes were reconstructed conservatively. Missing values were investigated where feasible, but intervals were not converted into invented point estimates and strain identities were not silently substituted.

Thermal envelope integrity

1,137 final records have complete thermal envelopes with zero ordering violations. Complete pH envelopes: 468, also with zero ordering violations.

03 · Evolution

Thermal position is strongly structured by lineage. Thermal breadth is different.

The phylogenetic analysis separates where an organism sits in thermal space from how wide its thermal envelope is.

Thermal traits

Tmin, Topt and Tmax: strong phylogenetic structure in bacterial and archaeal subsets.

Thermal breadth: substantially less phylogenetically structured.

Then we asked what shifts with thermal change.

34mapped ThermoBase tips across 8 major positive bacterial Topt-shift branches >10 °C

Selected descendants were enriched for fermentation, nitrate reduction and sulfur reduction, and were overwhelmingly aquatic. These are hypothesis-generating associations, not causal proof.

The complication

Ecology, metabolism and ancestry travel together. Can we separate them?

Planetary bridge

We can now distinguish useful environmental relationships from lineage shortcuts. What happens when we take those constraints beyond Earth?

05 · Apply

We leave Earth only after we know what the Earth data can actually support.

Planetary environments are treated as scenarios, not as one-dimensional “life scores.”

Different worlds, different evidence regimes

Strongest current ThermoBase case study, especially for localized hydrothermal-interface scenarios.
High chemical/redox relevance, but the relevant thermal regime is scenario-dependent.
Remote atmospheric interpretation is fundamentally different from direct ocean-world chemistry.

The output is not “habitability = 87%”. The output is: which observed terrestrial metabolic strategies are compatible with this scenario?

06 · Enceladus

The final test: biology versus chemistry.

Enceladus is where the entire chain comes together: environment → metabolism → chemistry → biosignature reasoning → abiotic challenge.

H₂ + CO₂ → CH₄

14 / 14hydrogenotrophic methanogenesis compatibility criteria

But 14/14 is not a probability of life. It is an ordinal compatibility score across the project's 14 criteria.

Attack the hypothesis

serpentinizationwater–rock chemistryabiotic carbon chemistryredox processes

A molecule is weak evidence. A coupled reaction network is stronger. The real target is a biological + geological + isotopic + chemical pattern that abiotic models cannot reproduce.

The ThermoBase conclusion: the strongest planetary use is not predicting where life is. It is identifying which metabolic hypotheses are chemically and physiologically plausible, then asking what observation would discriminate them from abiotic alternatives.
Final question

What measurement would make the biological and abiotic explanations diverge?

That is where ThermoBase ends — not with a life score, but with a better experiment.

The evidence layer

Now open the machinery.

The story layer is explanatory. The research layer remains exploratory: the 337-field schema, record-level evidence, provenance, QC exceptions, chemistry roles, phylogenetic mappings, ML diagnostics and planetary assumptions should remain available to a scientist who wants to interrogate the result.

What remains genuinely open?

Three methodological closure gates remain: the exact chemical-role adjudication decision procedure; the exact ML software/hyperparameters; and complete uncertainty intervals across the analytical program.

Scientific rule

Do not hide missingness, non-estimable models, lineage dependence or abiotic alternatives. They are part of the result.
