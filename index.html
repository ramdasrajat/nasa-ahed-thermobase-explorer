THE FIRST QUESTION

Do recurring physiological patterns reflect environmental constraints — or the lineages that happen to occupy those environments?

The next stages progressively add physiology, environment, chemistry and taxonomy before asking the machine-learning models to separate these effects.

09 · Planetary hypotheses

Take the framework beyond Earth — without pretending the worlds are equivalent.

Each world has a different evidence regime. Enceladus is a detailed case study; Europa is an ocean-world analogue; TRAPPIST-1 e and K2-18 b are remote atmospheric targets.

How to use this page

Choose a world above. Read the four boxes: what is observed, what ThermoBase contributes, what remains unknown, and what would discriminate the hypotheses. These worlds are deliberately not assigned one common “life score.”

Enceladus pathway discrimination

14 criteria · ordinal compatibility
Critical distinction

14/14 is not a probability of life. It means the pathway met all 14 criteria in the project’s discrimination matrix. The biological and abiotic hypotheses remain distinguishable only to the extent that the planetary constraints are known.

Enceladus: the reasoning chain

world-specific, explicit
Current scientific caution

Candidate biosignatures must be evaluated against abiotic baselines, planetary transport, temperature, chemistry and observational uncertainty. Molecules associated with life are not automatically evidence of life.

OBSERVED

What is strongest?

The database contains structured physiological and environmental observations across 1,238 records, with a much stronger thermal layer than pH or pressure coverage.

SUPPORTED

What survives analysis?

Several metabolic pathways retain useful environment signal under lineage-controlled validation, while hydrogen oxidation provides an important example of a signal that collapses under lineage control.

UNKNOWN

What remains open?

ThermoBase does not establish extraterrestrial life, a universal environmental law, or a definitive biosignature. Planetary inference remains constrained by abiotic chemistry and incomplete observations.

Research philosophy

why the limitations matter
Do not hide complexity; organize it.

ThermoBase is intentionally explicit about uncertainty, lineage confounding, incomplete evidence and abiotic alternatives. A scientific interface is useful when it lets the user see not only the attractive pattern, but also the test that could make that pattern fail.
