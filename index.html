The first question

Are recurring physiological patterns environmental — or are we mostly seeing the lineages that occupy those environments?

That question drives the rest of the journey. We first map physiology, then place it in environmental context, then inspect metabolism and chemistry, audit the evidence, and only then ask models to separate competing explanations.

What changes under lineage control?

ROC vs PR vs balanced accuracy

Extreme thermal niche classifier

Topt ≥ 80°C
Separate question

This classifier asks whether environmental/ecological variables can identify extreme thermal niches. It is not a proxy for “better life” or for habitability.

What the models actually tell us

Sulfate, sulfur and iron reduction + fermentation: useful environment signal persists under phylum holdout.

H₂ oxidation: random-CV signal collapses under lineage control (ROC-AUC 0.213). This is a scientifically important negative result.

Methanogenesis: random performance is extremely high, but phylum-grouped validation is not estimable because all 52 positives are in one phylum. It should not be presented as lineage-independent environmental prediction.

The planetary bridge

We can now distinguish a useful environmental relationship from a lineage shortcut.

That distinction matters when ThermoBase is used to reason about worlds where we cannot sample the organisms directly. The final step is therefore not “predict life,” but compare metabolic hypotheses against planetary constraints and abiotic alternatives.

08 · Planetary hypotheses

From terrestrial niches to other worlds.

This is not a probability-of-life dashboard. It is a transparent hypothesis framework: what is observed, what is inferred, what ThermoBase can contribute, and what remains unknown.

Why there is no common “chance of life” score

framework consistency
Interpretation

Enceladus is scored only within the separate 14-criterion pathway matrix. Europa, TRAPPIST-1 e and K2-18 b are not assigned comparable numbers because the required environmental and observational constraints are not equivalent. Earth is the known-life control.

Pathway discrimination — Enceladus

ordinal compatibility, not probability
What 14/14 means

Hydrogenotrophic methanogenesis satisfies all 14 criteria used in our pathway matrix. It does not mean a 100% chance of life, nor does it exclude abiotic methane.

Evidence ladder

observed → inferred → hypothesized

Enceladus reaction logic

conceptual network
Core reaction

4 H₂ + CO₂ → CH₄ + 2 H₂O. The strength of the hypothesis comes from coupling H₂ availability, carbon chemistry, terrestrial methanogenic analogues and competing abiotic pathways — not methane alone.

What would reduce uncertainty?

2026 biosignature framework
Latest caution

The 2026 abiotic-baseline framework emphasizes that isotopic and chiral biosignatures require better constraints on internal temperature, rheology, lithology, abiotic organic inventory and ocean transport.

End of the journey

ThermoBase does not end with a claim that life exists somewhere else.

It ends with a sharper question: which environmental, metabolic and chemical observations would make a biosignature hypothesis more discriminating — and which missing measurements currently prevent that conclusion?

09 · Synthesis & open questions

What survives the whole journey?

ThermoBase ends where a scientific investigation should end: not with a universal claim, but with a structured map of what the evidence supports, what the models add, where they fail, and which measurements would change the answer.

The ThermoBase reasoning chain

from database to biosignature
How to read this

The chain is deliberately directional: an environmental constraint can define a physiological niche; a niche can constrain plausible metabolisms; metabolism can imply chemical transformations; only then can those transformations become biosignature hypotheses — and only after comparison with abiotic alternatives.

Important limitations

do not overinterpret
Sampling structure

Taxonomy and environment are unevenly represented; random CV can therefore exaggerate generalization.

Missing physiology

pH and pressure are much less complete than temperature, so multidimensional niche maps represent measured subsets.

Phylogeny

No explicit ThermoBase-specific phylogenetic tree was built; lineage control uses phylum grouping rather than a causal evolutionary model.

Planetary thermodynamics

No first-principles Enceladus ΔG calculation was performed because relevant activities/fugacities and environmental states remain uncertain.

next scientific moves
The project conclusion

ThermoBase is strongest when it makes the uncertainty structure explicit.

Its value is not a single “habitability score.” It is the ability to connect a large terrestrial extremophile evidence base to environmental and metabolic hypotheses, test those relationships against lineage structure, and carry the surviving logic into planetary case studies without pretending that unknowns are known.
